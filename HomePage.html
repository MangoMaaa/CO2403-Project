What is Cyber Security?

Cyber Security refers to the study of defending computers, people and data from malicious actors who target systems for a variety of reasons. In our increasingly digital world, cybersecurity is not just an academic field of study, it's the backbone that supports the very threads of society. The threats we face are not reserved to large corporations or governments; they extend to every individual. From mere email scams to identity theft to breaches of medical equipment, cyber threats have the potence to destroy lifelines - literally.1





The threat of cyber threats is not just a far fetched fantasy, it is very much real and pressing matter. This live threat map from Kaspesky shows just how active and brutal the digital battlefield is. Every arrow and mark, dot and line, represents a cyber attack being identified in real time. According to study by the University of Maryland a cyber breach occurs every 39 seconds.2
As cyber secruity professionals, our job is to understand these attacks, develop tools and techniques to defend against them, to protect innocent people and systems. If you are curious about the realities of cyber safety and are keen to become part of the solution, it may be worthwile to consider this degree.


What do you need to know?

As tools, malwares and the threat landscape continuously evolve, humans remain as the weakest link. Every search, every click, every internet connection, every conversation can be breached and exploited. The global cyber security strategy is no longer a question of if cyber security attacks will occur - but when? The safety of your personal information lies in your hands. Even if you do not choose to pursue this as a degree, taking action to protect yourself online is critical. Below are some tips we believe you cannot afford to ignore.

Question Mark GIFfrom Question Mark GIFs


  • Strong passwords: Websites prompt you to create password using a mix of numeric letters for a reason. Brute force or dictionary attacks can figure out the name of your pet or favourite show in under half an hour. Do not compromise on the strength of your passwords.

  • Multi Factor Authentication - as tedious as it may be, multi factor authentication works and can be the difference between safe and compromised credentials.

  • Back Up your Data. Technology and humans are like are both very unreliable. Ransomware attacks can lock you out of important files and documents, in exchange for a horrendous sum, your data may or may not be returned.

  • Public Wifi,Public QR Codes - Do we even need to say it? As appealing as it maybe Public Wifi is a hackers gateway to easy interception. Avoid making purchases, accessing bank accounts and entering passwords on public wifi.

  • Never respond to unknown phone calls or emails. Legitimate contacts will follow up with you if necessary. Be cautious—phishing is one of the most common methods used to breach personal and sensitive information.
