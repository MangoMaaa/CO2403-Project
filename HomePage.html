Why Cyber Security?

Cyber Security refers to the study of defending computers, people and data from malicious actors who target systems for a variety of reasons. In our increasingly digital world, cybersecurity is not just an academic field of study, it's the backbone that supports the very threads of society. The threats we face are not reserved to large corporations or governments; they extend to every individual. From mere email scams to identity theft to breaches of medical equipment, cyber threats have the potence to destroy lifelines - literally.1
