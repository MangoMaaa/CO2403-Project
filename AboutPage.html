About the Team

In terms of other courses, Cyber Security is a very new one. With limited and conflicting opinions online constantly changing, it is understandable to feel lost or unsure about whether to pursue a degree in it and whether it will be worthwile. Below are some stories from students at UCLan that detail their reasons for choosing Cyber Security.


In one of our first lectures my lecturer made a statement: Oil is no longer the most valauble commodity - data is. I think that was one of the moments where it really clicked for me and i realised the true magnitude of the field I was entering. Cybersecurity has come to mean a lot more than protecting computers and passwords; it has become the means to safeguard the very fabric of the digital world. Whilst I was tremendously passionate about History I was not keen on pursuing it as a career, and felt massively disillusioned by the occupational routes Law and Politics offered as I failed to see the sincere change brought about by those in the positions I sought to follow. Although I wasnt a technical person, nor interested in much STEM subjects I was also massively paranoid. with the state of the world i was always indirectly concerned about cyber safety and would always try out for myself how much personal information attributed to my friends and family was publicly accessible on the internet and how much information we voluntarily shared out there. As exam results and university confirmations were nearing I was thinking more and more of studying cybersecurity but assumed it may be unfeasible to study as my a level subjects didnt seem to directly correspond with cybersecurity and was worried i would find it too challenging. Nevertheless I chose to perservere with my prefeered course and i can honestly say it was on of the best decisions I made.
