About the Team

In terms of other courses, Cyber Security is a very new one. With limited and conflicting opinions online constantly changing, it is understandable to feel lost or unsure about whether to pursue a degree in it and whether it will be worthwile. Below are some stories from students at UCLan that detail their reasons for choosing Cyber Security.


Student A

In one of our first lectures my lecturer made a statement: Oil is no longer the most valuable commodity - data is. Cybersecurity has come to mean a lot more than protecting computers and passwords; it has become the means to safeguard the very fabric of the digital world. Whilst I was tremendously passionate about History I was not keen on pursuing it as a career, and felt massively disillusioned by the occupational routes Law and Politics offered as I failed to see the sincere change brought about by those in the positions I sought to follow. Although I wasn't a technical person, I was also massively paranoid. With the state of the world I was always indirectly concerned about cyber safety and would always try out for myself how much personal information attributed to my friends and family was publicly accessible on the internet and how much information we voluntarily shared out there. As exam results and university confirmations were nearing I was afraid it may be unfeasible to study as my A-level subjects didn't seem to directly correspond with cybersecurity and was worried I would find it too challenging. Nevertheless I chose to persevere with my preferred course and I can honestly say it was one of the best decisions I made.

Person 2

I've always loved computers since I could remember. I never saw myself studying anything other than computer science. After I studied Computer Science A Levels which I enjoyed I still thought studying computer science at undergraduate level would also be repetitive. I decided to explore some more options. I went online and to the careers office at my college and that's when they suggested Cyber Security. I was a bit hesitant as it is a relatively new degree, I didn't know anyone who was studying it or was already in the field and was afraid and unsure whether this was the right career for me. Eventually after extensive research I decided to give it a go, unlike computer science I felt cyber security was a role that would always be needed no matter how far technology develops.

Person 4

Choosing to study cybersecurity was an easy decision for me because it combines intellectual challenge, real-world impact, and strong career prospects. The field is constantly evolving, requiring continuous learning to stay ahead of emerging threats, which excites me. My passion for problem-solving and ethical hacking drives me to understand how systems work and how to protect them. Additionally, cybersecurity offers diverse career paths and is in high demand, ensuring a stable and rewarding future. Most importantly, I find fulfillment in contributing to a safer digital world, protecting individuals, businesses, and governments from cyber threats.
