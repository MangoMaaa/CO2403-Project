What is an Insider Threat?

An insider threat is a threat that arises from any person who has or had authorised access to knowledge ofa comapanies resources, including personell, facilities, networks and systems, and has the potential to harm an organisation. According to a verizon data breach report, it was said that ~20% of breaches were caused by actors within an organisation. 1




Types of Insider Breaches:



Unintentional: Negligence

An insider that exposes an organisation to a cyber threat out of carelessnes or stupidity. However, negligent insiders are usually aware of Security policies yet they choose to ignore them out of laziness which can put the company at a great stake. Examples can include losing storage devices with important info, or ignoring security updates and alerts.

Unintentional: Accidental

Mistakenly casues an unintended risk to an organisation. This can include mistyping an email address, sending a business document to a competitor or accidentally clicking a hyperlink or attachment from a phishing email.

Intentional:

Often referred to as a Malicious Insider, intentional actions are performed with the comapanies worst interests at heart. Individuals usually act for personal benefit seeking monetary compensation or an alleviation of personal grievances. This can include leaking sensitive information, harrassing associates and stealing intellectul property in hope of faslely advancing their careers.

Intentional: Third Party

An individuls with no corporate or formal membership of an organisation who have been granted some access to facilities or systems. These threats can be direct or indirect and often times, these individuals may disguise the fact that they approached said company for the sole reason of gathering information to work against them.
